SUNSET BAY

AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Danny Vega is back in Sunset Bay with empty pockets and a long memory. Lena Marlowe has three jobs for him — steal a car, run her packages, and lose the heat. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, outrun the Sunset Bay PD, and earn your way back to the top.

WASD / Arrows move & drive  ·  Mouse drag camera  ·  Shift run  ·  E steal / exit car  ·  Click shoot  ·  Space brake / jump  ·  P pause  ·  M mute
An original game. Sunset Bay, its people and its story are invented — no real city, no real gang, no borrowed names.

HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD moves, Shift runs, mouse drag turns the camera, click fires your pistol, Space jumps.
In a car: walk up to any car and press E to take it. W/S accelerate and reverse, A/D steer, Space brakes, E gets out.
Wanted: stealing a car, hitting a pedestrian or ramming a police car raises your wanted level. Police chase and ram you. Break line of sight and stay hidden to cool down.
Missions: follow the yellow marker on the map and the HUD. Finish all three jobs to complete the story.
Save: progress saves automatically. CONTINUE on the menu picks up where you left off.
